## 調查報告
1. Report on the New Terrories, 1899-1912.
::: note 引文
The only Potteries are at Wun Iu, near Taipo, about 400,000 pots, rice bowls and plates are here turned out every year, of an average value of 6 cash each; most of them are exported to Tam Shui in Chinese Territory; some also to Hongkong.
:::
